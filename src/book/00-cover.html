A book in thirteen chapters

Dams in Section

From a handful of sand to a dam you design

Soil holds only as well as its grains press on one another, and the water between them can take that press away.

A dam is a heap of earth and rock, or a block of concrete, that holds back water or mine waste. This book cuts dams open the way engineers draw them, in section, and asks of each one the two questions in the sentence above. It starts with a handful of sand on a beach and ends with a dam you design and test yourself. Most of the drawings are live. Drag them, play them, and try to break them.
