Plate 0 · How to read a section

A section is what you would see if you cut a dam straight across with a very large knife, lifted one half away and looked at the cut face, the way you look at the layers in a slice of cake. Engineers draw dams this way because the things that decide whether a dam stands (how deep the water is against it, where water seeps through it, which slope might slide) all show in that one cut, and along most of a long dam one slice looks much like the next. So the book works per metre of dam: picture a slice one metre thick.
